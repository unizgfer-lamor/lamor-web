TIAGo OMNI Base is an omnidirectional mobile base with four mecanum wheels and shock absorbers, so it can drive in any direction and position itself precisely in confined indoor spaces. Two LiDARs give an unobstructed 360° view for mapping, localization and obstacle avoidance, complemented by an IMU and wheel odometry. The base measures 50 × 72 × 31 cm, weighs 55 kg, carries up to 100 kg, reaches 1.5 m/s and runs 8–10 hours on its 36 V Li-ion battery. It runs Ubuntu and ROS with PAL's navigation software, and a Gazebo simulation model is available. The base is suited to research on navigation, visual odometry and indoor logistics.
