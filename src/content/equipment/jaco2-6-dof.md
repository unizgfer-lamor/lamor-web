Kinova’s robot arms are the perfect solution for integrating a manipulation dimension to mobile platforms. They are the ideal partners to assist or replace technicians and professionals for inspection or manipulation in harsh, confined, hazardous or manufacturing environments. Light and compact, the arm can be integrated with the majority of existing mobile platform models. Moreover, due to its low power consumption, the robot arm will not significantly reduce the autonomy of the platform.
