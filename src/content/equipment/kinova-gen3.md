Gen3 is a lightweight robot arm with a carbon-fiber and aluminum shell, available with 7 or 6 degrees of freedom. Each joint actuator measures torque as well as motor current and temperature, and the arm is controlled over Ethernet at 1 kHz, which suits it to compliant manipulation, physical human–robot interaction and mounting on mobile platforms. The 7-DoF version weighs 8.2 kg, reaches 902 mm and carries up to 4 kg at mid-range or 2 kg at full reach. An optional vision module at the wrist combines an RGB camera with an Intel RealSense depth sensor. The arm is programmed through the Kinova Kortex API (C++, Python, MATLAB) and ROS.
