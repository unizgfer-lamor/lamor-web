SO-ARM101 (SO-101) is a low-cost, open-source robot arm designed by TheRobotStudio together with Hugging Face for the LeRobot library. Its 3D-printed body carries six Feetech STS3215 serial bus servos: five drive the arm joints (shoulder pan, shoulder lift, elbow flex, wrist flex and wrist roll) and one drives the gripper. The arm is designed for a leader–follower setup in which a person moves the leader arm by hand and the follower arm copies its motion. This is used for teleoperation and for recording demonstrations to train imitation-learning policies with LeRobot. The hardware design is published under the Apache 2.0 license.
