Insta360 X4 is a compact dual-lens 360° camera with 1/2-inch sensors. It records 360° video at up to 8K 30 fps or 5.7K 60 fps and 72 MP 360° photos, and in single-lens mode it records conventional video at up to 4K 60 fps. A six-axis gyroscope provides FlowState stabilization and 360° horizon lock. The camera weighs 203 g, is waterproof to 10 m without a housing and records for about 135 minutes at 5.7K 30 fps on its 2290 mAh battery. Its full spherical coverage is useful for recording robot experiments and for collecting panoramic image data.
