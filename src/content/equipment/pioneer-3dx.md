The Pioneer is the world's most popular research mobile robot. The Pioneer’s versatility, reliability and durability have made it the reference platform for robotics research. The Pioneer includes a dedicated motion controller with encoder feedback and is fully programmable. It is perfect for mapping / localization experiments.
