Artificial intelligence in control systems and robotics means making advanced control decisions autonomously, based on models, i.e. taking into account predictions of the processes within the system. This relies on time series forecasting, a specialised branch of machine learning for technical systems that builds on the historical measurements and database archives collected in digitalised and automated dynamic processes in control and robotics. Students will acquire the knowledge and skills to analyse, process and visualise data, and to propose and adapt machine learning approaches and model structures for forecasting future trends of variables and classifying events in technical systems. Demonstration lectures use datasets of historical real-world process measurements from business and infrastructure dynamic systems (process automation, transport, buildings) and from robotic systems. In a Python environment, students will go through all key phases of time series forecasting, from data preprocessing and analysis, through model tuning, evaluation and error analysis, to the final implementation on a central or embedded computer for autonomous decision making. The lectures are reinforced by practical laboratory exercises on examples of dynamic systems from control and robotics. Students will master the basics of time series analysis, learn how to discover patterns in data and build predictive models using statistical methods and machine learning.
