  LAMOR, together with the project partner Končar Electrical Engineering Institute, has received a European Regional Development Fund grant. SafeTRAM, or *System for Increased driving safety in public urban rail traffic,* is a project aiming to develop an innovative system which would increase traffic safety by implementing safety functions for urban rail traffic, thus lowering the number of accidents causing great material damage and human casualties. For more details please visit our research projects [page](/research/projects/).
