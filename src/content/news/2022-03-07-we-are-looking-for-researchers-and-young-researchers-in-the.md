  We are looking for researchers and young researchers to join us in developing technologies for autonomous robotics systems in the project "A-UNIT - Research and development of an advanced unit for autonomous management of mobile vehicles in logistics".

  Candidates for researchers should have a master's degree in computer science, electrical engineering, mathematics, mechanical engineering or other related fields. Candidates for young researchers should have a master's degree or be students of the final semester of the study of computer science, electrical engineering, mathematics, mechanical engineering or other related fields.

  Interested candidates should contact the A-UNIT project leader, prof. dr. sc. Ivan Petrović, to the e-mail ivan.petrovic@fer.hr, no later than March 14, 2022.
