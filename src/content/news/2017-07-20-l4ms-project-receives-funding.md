  LAMOR, together with several European partners, has received a Factories of the Future Innovation Action grant. L4MS, or Logistics for Manufacturing SMEs, is a project coordinated by the VTT Technical Research Centre of Finland. The main goal of the project is disruptive innovation for European SMEs & Mid-Caps by building on their intra-factory logistics challenge and unleashing their digitalization potential by powering new robot systems that are more cost effective at lower lot sizes. For more details please visit our research projects [page](/research/projects/).
