  The recipient of the University of Zagreb **Fran Bošnjaković award for 2021** is LAMOR’s Prof. Ivan Petrović who got acknowledged for his scientific achievements, promotion of the science, his contribution in the exchange of knowledge and especially for education of young professionals. Congratulations to Prof. Petrović!

  This award has been [awarded](http://www.unizg.hr/istrazivanje/istrazivanje-i-inovacije/nagrade-za-posebna-postignuca/fran-bosnjakovic/) by The University of Zagreb for the achievements in the technical fields since 1994.
