  LAMOR's Fabio Bonsignorio and Ivan Petrović participated in European Robotics Forum 2025 ([ERF '25](https://erf2025.eu/)), which is taking place in Stuttgart from 25 to 27 of March.

  In scope of the conference, ERA Chair Fabio Bonsignorio co-organized the Workshop: Test before Invest! Reproducibility, Benchmarking, and Competitions as enablers of technology transfer and held a lecture titled "Why Reproducibility does matter ?".

  Prof Ivan Petrović presented a poster on "Object Position Estimation Using Electromagnetic Phenomena of Flexible Metasurfaces".
