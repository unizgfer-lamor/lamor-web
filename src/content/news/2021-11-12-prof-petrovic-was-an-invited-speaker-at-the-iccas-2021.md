  On October 15, 2021 Prof. Petrović held an invited lecture titled “Autonomy and interaction capabilities of robots in performing complex tasks” at **the 21<sup>st</sup> International Conference on Control, Automation and Systems [(ICCAS 2021)](http://2021.iccas.org/?page_id=1312)**, addressing the main robotics challenges in the next few decades - development of autonomous robotic systems that can perform complex tasks in human environments and safely cooperate with humans. In his talk Professor Petrović also presented LAMOR’s recent research activities and achievements and discussed open research challenges and potential directions for future research.
