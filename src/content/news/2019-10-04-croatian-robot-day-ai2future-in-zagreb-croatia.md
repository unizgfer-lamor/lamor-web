  We invite you to join us for this year's **Croatian Robot Day** taking place on October 10, 2019 as part of the largest conference on business AI applications in Croatia - [AI2FUTURE](http://ai2future.com/). Co-organizers of this part of the programme, that will focus on robotics in logistics and agile production, are Zagreb's Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER) and Innovation Centre Nikola Tesla (ICENT).

  Programme will be presented and moderated by FER's professors Ivan Petrović and Ivan Marković, and alogside lectures from world experts **Achim J. Lilienthal**, University of Örebro, Sweden, and **Björn Hein,** Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, Germany, you will also have the opportunity to hear Croatian examples of companies that have made a strong leap in this area - [Gideon Brothers](https://www.gideonbros.ai/) and [RoMb Technologies](https://www.linkedin.com/company/romb-technologies/). Furthermore, R&I activities of FER in this domain will be presented together with possibilities of equity free financing for robotics projects from EU H2020 funds.

  You can find the detailed programme at the following [link](http://ai2future.com/program-2019-robot-day/).

  Participation at the Croatian Robot Day, taking place at the KRAŠ Auditorium in Zagreb, is free of charge under the condition of mandatory registration at the following [link](http://ai2future.com/events/artificial-intelligence-future-2019/.).

  In order to participate at the whole AI2FUTURE conference, registration fee is mandatory.
