  **Ivan Petrović** and **Marija Seder**, together with ICENT's Technology Monitoring and Market Analysis Manager Dr. Davorka Moslavac, attended the kick-off meeting of our new project titled Logistics for Manufacturing SMEs (L4MS). The objective of the project is to facilitate the take up of logistics automation by manufacturing SMEs across Europe by acting as a broker between SMEs, who seek solutions and financing, and the established Competence Centres and their ecosystem, who are interested to provide consulting, solutions, best practices and areas for demonstration and experimentation.
