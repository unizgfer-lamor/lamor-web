  The projects [AIFORS - ERA Chair in Artificial Intelligence for Robotics](https://aifors.fer.hr/aifors) and [DATACROSS – Advanced Methods and Technologies in Data Science and Cooperative Systems](https://www.fer.unizg.hr/datacross), in collaboration with [OSRF - Open Source Robotics Foundation](https://www.openrobotics.org/), are organizing the workshop **ROSCon Croatia 2026.**\

  The workshop will be held on Monday, March 30, 2026, in the Grey Hall of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (UNIZG-FER). The workshop is open to all interested researchers and engineers from academia and industry who register by Monday, March 16, 2026, at 12:00, using the form at the [following link](https://forms.office.com/e/yFBfCM6uSn).\

  We invite all those interested in presenting their work in the context of ROS to register for a lecture by March 13, 2026. The workshop will be held in Croatian.\

  More details about the workshop, including instructions for registering for a lecture, are available at the [following link](https://roscon.ros.org/hr).
