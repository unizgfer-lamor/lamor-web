  Centre of Excellence Research Unit for Advanced Cooperative Systems ACROSS organize international scientific workshop

  **„2nd ACROSS Workshop on Advanced Cooperative Systems“**

  The workshop will be held on **November 29 (Tuesday)**, **2016,** starting **at 09:00 in Grey Hall** at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER).

  Technical program contains invited lectures given by renowned experts about about top research activities in the field of advance cooperative control systems and its applications. Research activities of IJ ACROSS members in this field will be presented through the poster session.

  The workshop is **open to all** interested participants, with **obligatory prior notification** on e-mail [mirjana.stjepanovic@fer.hr](mailto:mirjana.stjepanovic@fer.hr), no later than November 24 (Thursday), 2016 until 14:00.

  Agenda of the workshop is now **available** in the detailed news content
