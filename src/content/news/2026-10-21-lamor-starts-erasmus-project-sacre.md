  On 1 October 2026 LAMOR started the Erasmus+ cooperation partnership **SACRE - Senior Advancement in Collaborative Robotics and Education**, coordinated by the University of Bremen together with CETEM (Spain), Bimec (Bulgaria) and the University of Zagreb Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing.

  Over the next two years, SACRE will help workers aged 55+ in manufacturing keep and advance their jobs by building competences in collaborative robotics, automation and digitalisation. More about the project is on [our projects page](/research/projects/#sacre).
---

Automation is changing many job roles in manufacturing, and older workers are often the least supported in that transition. SACRE will first analyse these changes and identify the job roles affected by automation, leading to a validated competence framework for collaborative robotics.

Building on the framework, the partners will design and pilot a competence-based training programme for workers aged 55+, develop interactive online modules hosted on a learning management system, and structure micro-credentials aligned with the European Qualifications Framework, so that the training can be recognised within vocational education and training systems.

The project is funded by the Erasmus+ programme (KA220-VET, Cooperation partnerships in vocational education and training) and runs from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2028. At LAMOR, the project team includes Fabio Bonsignorio, Ivan Petrović, Marija Seder and Ivan Marković.
