  On November 26, 2021, at the Day of FER ceremony, **Juraj Peršić** was awarded FER's **Silver Plaque “Josip Lončar”** for his outstanding doctoral dissertation and research work titled „Extrinsic and temporal calibration of heterogenous exteroceptive mobile sensor systems“ under the mentorship of Prof. Petrović.

  Juraj's work was also recognized by the industry which awarded him the **„Končar“ Award,** given annually for the doctoral dissertation with an outstanding research achievement for industry application. Congratulations to Juraj!
