  Prof. Petrović participated at **the Central European Technology Forum** ([CETEF'22](https://cetef.eu/en/main/)) in Wroclaw on October 25, 2022, as an invited keynote speaker with the lecture “Mobile Robotics and AI in Robotisation of the European Industry”.

  The lecture was held within the scope of the thematic conference on Development trends, opportunities, and barriers of robotization of the European industry and the main subject of the session dedicated to automation and robotization of production processes was the methods to overcome challenges posed to the industry.
