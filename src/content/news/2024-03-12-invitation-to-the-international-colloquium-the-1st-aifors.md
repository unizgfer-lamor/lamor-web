lead: |-
  The project **AIFORS - ERA Chair in Artificial Intelligence for Robotics** and **LAMOR - Laboratory for autonomous systems and mobile robotics** organize an international scientific colloquium

  **The 1st AIFORS Colloquium on Natural and Artificial Intelligence**

  The colloquium will be held on Tuesday, **March 19, 2024**, **in the Gray Hall of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing** (UNIZG-FER).

  The technical program of the colloquium contains four invited lectures that will be given by renowned international scientists in the field of artificial intelligence and robotics, as well as lectures by the ERA Chair holder of the AIFORS project.

  **Participation in the colloquium is open** to all interested researchers from the academic community and industry **who register by Monday, March 18, 2024, noon,** via the following [**link**](https://sites.google.com/view/aifors-colloquium-2024/registration).

  The colloquium program is available on the [**colloquium website**](https://sites.google.com/view/aifors-colloquium-2024/home).
