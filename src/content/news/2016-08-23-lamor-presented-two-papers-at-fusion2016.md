[**Moving object tracking employing rigid body motion on matrix Lie groups**](http://bib.irb.hr/prikazi-rad?&rad=815302) // *19th International Conference on Information Fusion (FUSION), Special Session on Directional Estimation*. Heidelberg, Germany, 2016.
  - Marković, Ivan; Ćesić, Josip; Petrović, Ivan. [**On wrapping the Kalman filter and estimating with the SO(2) group**](http://bib.irb.hr/prikazi-rad?&rad=815300) // *19th International Conference on Information Fusion (FUSION), Special Session on Directional Estimation*. Heidelberg, Germany, 2016.
