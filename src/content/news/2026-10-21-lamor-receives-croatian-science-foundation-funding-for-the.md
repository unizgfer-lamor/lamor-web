  Over the next 3 years, LAMOR will conduct the scientific project **STAMP - Stochastic integrated task and motion planning for human-robot collaboration**, funded by the Croatian Science Foundation under the Installation Research Projects (UIP) programme and led by Assistant Professor Luka Petrović.

  Project STAMP aims to enable robot arms to safely and efficiently collaborate with humans in dynamic and uncertain environments by unifying high-level task planning and low-level motion planning within a single stochastic framework. More information about the project is available at [**the project website**](https://stamp.fer.hr/).
