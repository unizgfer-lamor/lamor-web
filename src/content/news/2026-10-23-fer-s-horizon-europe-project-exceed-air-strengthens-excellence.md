  The European project **[EXCEED-AIR](/research/projects/#exceed-air) – EXCellence Initiative for European Empowerment and Development in AI-Driven Robotics** began on 1 October 2026 at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER), University of Zagreb. Funded by the *Horizon Europe* programme under the *European Excellence Initiative*, the project brings together ten partner organisations from six European countries. With a total budget of **€5 million**, the project will run for five years, until 30 September 2031. FER coordinates the project, which is led by **Academician Professor Ivan Petrović, PhD**.

  The project's first consortium meeting was held in mid-October at FER's MARBLE Centre of Excellence, marking the beginning of joint work among the partners.
---

The main objective of EXCEED-AIR is to establish a long-term, sustainable European alliance of excellence in AI-driven robotics. By connecting research teams, infrastructure and expertise, the project will strengthen the scientific and innovation capacities of partner institutions, particularly in countries covered by widening participation and excellence measures, while promoting the uptake of research results in industry and society.

## Strengthening Research Collaboration and Infrastructure

Project activities include improving research assessment and career development, strengthening research management, providing professional development, mentoring, and international mobility opportunities for researchers and professional staff. The partners will develop a joint research and innovation programme and establish a shared virtual laboratory connecting their research platforms and enabling experiments to be conducted across multiple locations. The project will also develop digital collaboration infrastructure, digital twins, and tools for secure data sharing and research reproducibility.

## From Advanced Algorithms to Real-World Robotics Applications

Joint research will focus on four strategic domains of AI application in robotics:

- Robust Perception and Situational Awareness
- Learning and Adaptation in Unstructured Environments
- Human-Aware Real-Time Planning and Decision-Making
- Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Systems

The methods and algorithms developed will be applied and validated in assistive robotics for aging and healthcare, smart manufacturing and industrial automation, as well as maritime systems for environmental monitoring and infrastructure inspection. Particular emphasis will be placed on human-centred robotics, trustworthy AI, data protection and open science.

## A European Consortium of Leading Research Institutions

In addition to FER as project coordinator, the consortium includes the Jožef Stefan Institute (JSI) in Slovenia; the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering and Naval Architecture, University of Zagreb (FSB), in Croatia; KTH Royal Institute of Technology (KTH) in Sweden; the Technical University of Munich (TUM) and the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in Germany; the University of Padua (UNIPD) in Italy; the Croatian Academy of Sciences and Arts (HAZU) and the MARBLE Centre of Excellence in Croatia; and European Fund Management Consulting (EFMC) in Estonia.

EXCEED-AIR will also foster collaboration with companies, startups, regional innovation ecosystems and citizens through the co-design of applications, demonstrations of robotic technologies and public engagement activities. In doing so, the project will raise the international profile of FER and its partner institutions while laying the foundations for lasting European cooperation in artificial intelligence and robotics.
