  LAMOR's Prof. Petrović is the coordinator of the recently accepted first Croatian WIDESPREAD Teaming Phase I project. The overall objective is to create a Centre of Excellence for Autonomous and Cooperative Robotic Systems in Croatia, which will be at the forefront of research and innovation of novel methodologies and advanced engineering approaches in the targeted domains. The Centre will be created and run as a long-term joint venture between the University of Zagreb Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing, the Royal Institute of Technology, and the Innovation Centre Nikola Tesla.
