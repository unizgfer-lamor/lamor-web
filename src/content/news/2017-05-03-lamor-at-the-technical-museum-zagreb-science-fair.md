  Together with the [Laboratory for Robotics and Intelligent Control Systems - LARICS](http://larics.rasip.fer.hr/) and the [Laboratory for Underwater Systems and Technologies - LABUST](http://labust.fer.hr), LAMOR presented robotics research at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (**robotics@fer.hr**) at the [Science Fair](http://www.festivalznanosti.hr/2017/) organized by the Technical Museum Nikola Tesla in Zagreb. The Science Fair took place from 24th until 29th April 2017. LAMOR's Ivan Marković also held a lecture for children under the title "Robots, androids and artificial intelligence through movies and cartoons" and gave an interview for the Croatian radio station [Yammat.fm](http://yammat.fm/).
