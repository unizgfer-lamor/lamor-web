  Croatian Academy of Sciences and Arts held a ceremonial session during which [Academy Awards](http://info.hazu.hr/upload/File/2018-2/DEPLIJAN-NAGRADE-HAZU-2018.pdf) for highest scientific and arts achievements in 2017 were awarded. For the area of technical sciences the award was given to LAMOR's Prof. Ivan Petrović for research in autonomous navigation of mobile robots in unknown and dynamic environments. Congratulations to Prof. Petrović!
