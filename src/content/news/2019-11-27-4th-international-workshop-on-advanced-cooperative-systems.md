  Project **DATACROSS** – Advanced methods and technologies in Data Science and Cooperative Systems and **The Centre of Research Excellence for Data Science and Advanced Cooperative Systems**, research unit **ACROSS**, are organizing the following international scientific workshop taking place on November 28 and 29 at UNIZG-FER:

  **"4th International Workshop on Advanced Cooperative Systems"**

  Technical programme includes 4 invited lectures by renowned international researchers and 4 lectures by ACROSS leading researchers. We will also have a section of lectures given by ACROSS young researchers. Workshop is open to all interested scientists and doctoral students. Registration is free, but mandatory.

  More information about the workshop and the registration form are available at the following [link](http://sites.google.com/view/iwacs-2019/homepage).
