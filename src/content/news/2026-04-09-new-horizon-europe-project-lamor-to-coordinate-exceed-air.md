  A new **5-year Horizon Europe European Excellence Initiative (EEI)** project titled **EXCEED-AIR - EXCellence Initiative for European Empowerment and Development in AI-Driven Robotics** has been accepted for financing, with UNIZG-FER being the coordinator and with Institut Jozef Stefan, Slovenia, UNIZG-FSB, Croatia, Kungliga Tekniska Högskolan, Sweden, Technische Universität München, Germany, Karlsruher Institut Für Technologie, Germany, Università degli Studi Di Padova, Italy, HAZU, Croatia, Centar izvrsnosti MARBLE, Croatia and European Fund Management Consulting, Estonia as project partners.

  This *Coordination and Support Action (CSA)* project, financed under *the HORIZON-WIDERA-2025-01-ACCESS-01-EEI* program, will be implemented over 5 years with a budget of 5 million EUR.
