  Prof. Fabio Bonsignorio has recently joined LAMOR and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing (FER) having been appointed for the position of ERA Chair Holder following an international open call. As a part of the project AIFORS, he will lead the Research Group for Artificial Intelligence for Robotics, assigned to the FER's LAMOR lab, which aims to take intelligent soft robotics to the next stage complementing the existing expertise and research direction of the LAMOR lab. We are happy to have Prof. Bonsignorio onboard!
