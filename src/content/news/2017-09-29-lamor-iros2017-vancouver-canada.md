  **Ivan Marković** and **Josip Ćesić** have attended the **IEEE/RJS International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems** (IROS2017), September 24-28, Vancouver, Canada, with two papers presenting recent research results

  - Lenac, Kruno; Ćesić, Josip; Marković, Ivan; Cvišić, Igor; Petrović, Ivan. [**Revival of filtering based SLAM? Exactly sparse delayed state filter on Lie groups**](http://bib.irb.hr/prikazi-rad?&rad=884260) // *Proceedings of the IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS 2017)*. 2017.
  - Joukov, Vladimir; Ćesić, Josip; Westermann, Kevin; Marković, Ivan; Kulić, Dana; Petrović, Ivan. [**Human motion estimation on Lie groups using IMU measurements**](http://bib.irb.hr/prikazi-rad?&rad=884264) // *Proceedings of the IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS 2017)*. 2017.

  The second paper presented at the conference represents the result of a close collaboration with partners from the University of Waterloo. It was prepared in co-autorship of groups of Prof. Ivan Petrović and Prof. Dana Kulić from the University of Waterloo.
