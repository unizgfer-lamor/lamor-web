  LAMOR attended the 11<sup>th</sup> International Conference on Automation, Robotics, and Applications ([ICARA 2025](https://www.icara.us/)), which took place from February 12 to 14 in Zagreb.

  As a keynote speaker, prof Ivan Marković gave the lecture **Towards reliable long-term robot autonomy in challenging environments**, while researchers Borna Paro and Karlo Koledić presented the articles:

  - Borna Paro, Jelena Rogina, Luka Petrović, Marija Seder, Ivan Marković: **Coordinated Planning and Control of Two Mobile Robots for Joint Cargo Transportation**
  - Muhammad Awais, Karlo Koledić, Luka Petrović, Ivan Marković: **Enhancing Gaussian Splatting SLAM with Feature-based Tracking**
