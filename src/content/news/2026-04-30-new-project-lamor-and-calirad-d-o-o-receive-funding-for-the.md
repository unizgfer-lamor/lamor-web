  Over the next 3 years, LAMOR will conduct the collaborative scientific project **SIMMAP - Simulation-augmented multisensor environment modeling for operational safety in dynamic driving** in cooperation with the company Calirad d.o.o. as the project coordinator.

  Project SIMMAP aims to develop high-fidelity, interactive environmental models by processing multisensor data from road vehicles in challenging scenarios such as rally racing. Using advanced techniques such as SLAM and neural rendering, these models will enhance autonomous vehicle localization, simulate "what-if" safety scenarios, and create realistic training frameworks for both racing and everyday driving.
