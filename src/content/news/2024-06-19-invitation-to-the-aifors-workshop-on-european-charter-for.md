  The **AIFORS Lab - ERA Chair in Artificial Intelligence for Robotics** and **LAMOR - Laboratory for autonomous systems and mobile robotics** organize a workshop

  **The AIFORS Workshop on European Charter for Researchers and the Code of Conduct for the Recruitment of Researchers and Gender Analysis in Research**

  The workshop will be held on Thursday, **July 4, 2024**, **in the Gray Hall of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing** (UNIZG-FER) with the following **[agenda](/_news/125267/AIFORS_WS%20on%20ECCC%20-%20Researchers%20and%20GEP_04-07-2024%20-%20Program_FINAL.pdf)**.

  The morning session is to discuss the European Charter of Researchers and its role in fostering the creation of a fully functional, globally competitive, just and inclusive European Research Area. The afternoon session is dedicated to assess the current situation, discuss how to make further progress on gender equality and inclusion issues in research, globally, in the ERA, and in particular at the University of Zagreb and FER.

  Participation in the workshop is open to all interested attendees who register **by noon, Monday, July 1, 2024**, via the following **[link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/12eEua0aW89IivXIu8Bj9yNBSI1Mqu3bnDVzRAsVhoBA/viewform?edit_requested=true)**.
