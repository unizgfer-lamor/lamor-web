  LAMOR is proud to share the news that Prof. Ivan Petrović just became a new *Editor-In-Chief* of the *Robotics and Autonomous Systems* journal. RAS publishes fundamental developments in the field of **robotics**, with special emphasis on **autonomous systems**. An important goal of this journal is to extend the state of the art in both **symbolic** and **sensory** based **robot control** and **learning** in the context of autonomous systems. The Editorial board can be found at the following [link](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/robotics-and-autonomous-systems/about/editorial-board).
