  LAMOR will be taking part in [ERW 2018](https://www.eu-robotics.net/robotics_week/) under the **robotics@fer.hr** brand, together with our colleagues from [LABUST](https://labust.fer.hr/zari/labust) and [LARICS](https://larics.fer.hr)! We will participate in the [University of Zagreb Fair](http://smotra.unizg.hr/fileadmin/smotra2018/index.html) (22-24 November 2018) and present our work, equipment, projects, as well as opportunities for potential undergraduate, graduate, and PhD students, researchers, teachers etc. Also, our laboratory will be open to the public during the **Open Day event at UNIZG-FER** (24th November 2018) and will show the visitors what it is like to work in our three laboratories. We are looking forward to these events and hope to see you there!
