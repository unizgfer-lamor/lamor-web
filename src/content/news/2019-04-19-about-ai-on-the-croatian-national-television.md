  On April 8, 2019 the Croatian National Television show "Dobar dan Hrvatska" featured LAMOR's Prof. Ivan Petrović. The topic was on AI and the show was motivated by the next day's round table "EU Declaration on Cooperation on Artificial Intelligence - how to harmonize actions in Croatia" that took place on April 9, 2019 and was organized by the Croatian Academy of Sciences and The Centre of Research Excellence for Data Science and Advanced Cooperative Systems.

  The show is available [online](http://hrti.hrt.hr/videostore/moviedetails?referenceId=416255046&refer=search|index&customCatalogueReferenceId=search&player=False) via the HRTi service.
