  The International Science Festival in Gothenburg (Sweden) is one of Europe’s leading popular science events. Theme of the Festival for this year is Astonishing thought. Participants are challenged with questions: what happens when we transfer the thinking process to machines and robots, and the I is no longer always human? What happens if the machines become more intelligent than we are? Professor Ivan Petrović and PhD student Tomislav Petković gave their answers and presented LAMOR's smart robots at the Festival.
