  Laboratory for Autonomous Systems and Mobile Robotics (LAMOR, [http://lamor.fer.hr](/)) is seeking a researcher, senior researcher, lead researcher, and teaching/research assistant to join our team in the development of new technologies for autonomous systems and mobile robots.

  We offer the opportunity to work on EU-funded projects and industrial research projects in the fields of motion planning for robotic systems with a high number of degrees of freedom, estimation and control of robotic systems using visual feedback, as well as sensor fusion for environmental perception in mobile robots and autonomous vehicles.

  Candidates should hold a Master’s degree or be final-semester students in computer science, electrical engineering, information and communication technology, mathematics, physics, mechanical engineering, or related fields.

  Selected candidates who do not yet hold a PhD degree are expected to enroll in a doctoral study program at FER.

  All interested candidates are invited to contact Ivan Petrović, Ivan Marković, and Luka Petrović via e-mail at: ivan.petrovic@fer.hr, ivan.markovic@fer.hr, luka.petrovic@fer.hr.
