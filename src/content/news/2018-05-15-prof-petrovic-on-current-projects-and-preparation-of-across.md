  Prof. Petrović gave an interview for Croatian newspapers "Jutarnji list" on current projects. So far, Prof. Petrovic led more than 30 domestic and 20 international scientific projects, so it could be said that he is the *spiritus movens* of networking of the University of Zagreb Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing with European scientific institutions and companies. Prof. Petrović is currently involved in the preparation of a capital project worth EUR 35 million - a project aiming at establishing a Center of Excellence for Autonomous and Cooperative Robotic Systems - ACROSS, a jointly promising undertaking with the Royal Institute of Technology (KHT) from Stockholm.
