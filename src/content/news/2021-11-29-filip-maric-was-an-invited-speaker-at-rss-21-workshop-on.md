  LAMOR's Filip Marić was an invited speaker at the **Robotics: Science and Systems Workshop on Geometry and Topology in Robotics** [**(RSS '21)**](https://sites.google.com/view/geotopo-rss2021/home) held on July 15, 2021. In his lecture titled "Of Ellipsoids and Distances: Reconsidering Robot Kinematics" he discussed how advances in computational geometry open up new perspectives for reasoning about robot kinematics.

  You can see his presentation [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a2pG11CV_ug).
