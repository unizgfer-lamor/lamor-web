  The **AIFORS Lab - ERA Chair in Artificial Intelligence for Robotics** and **LAMOR - Laboratory for autonomous systems and mobile robotics,** in collaboration with EDIH Crobohub++ organize a workshop

  **The 1<sup>st</sup> AIFORS Workshop on Industry – Academia Collaboration**

  The workshop will be held on Tuesday, **July 9, 2024**, **in the Gray Hall of the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing** (UNIZG-FER) with the following [**agenda**](/_news/125545/AIFORS_Industry-academia%20WS%2009-07-2024_Program%20FINAL.pdf).

  Speakers with rich international experience in research and development in robotics and artificial intelligence will present their past collaborations with industry and the latest research and views on the future.

  Participation in the workshop is open to all interested attendees who register **by noon, Friday, July 5, 2024**, via the following [**link**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd7rrlnW3pDSbeCAsIy5cXXHm0Ra3ThSaL3SRxSKoukPNzDMA/viewform?vc=0&c=0&w=1&flr=0).
