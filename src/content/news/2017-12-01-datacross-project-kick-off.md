  Centre of Research Excellence for Data Science and Advanced Cooperative Systems (DATACROSS) was presented at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing of University of Zagreb. Within the project more than 40 new researchers will be employed. The Centre comprises two research units: the **Research Unit for Data Science** (RU DATASCIENCE, coordinated by Prof. Sven Lončarić) and the **Research Unit for Cooperative Systems** (RU ACROSS coordinated by Prof. Ivan Petrović).
