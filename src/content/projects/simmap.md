For autonomous systems, building an environment map represents a key step towards achieving full autonomy. In the SIMMAP project, we propose the development of environment models using a multisensor setup in challenging scenarios, such as rally competitions, where the environment will be represented through interactive simulations. These models have significant research and commercial potential, as they enable the augmentation of real-world data for deep learning models, the development of a new generation of environment models for decision-making, the identification of hazardous zones for spectators along the track, as well as realistic preparation and simulation of unexpected events during a race. Although developed in the context of rally racing, the SIMMAP methods will also be applicable as frameworks for the validation of autonomous systems and road safety.
