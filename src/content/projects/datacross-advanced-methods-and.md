DATACROSS is a project of the Centre of Research Excellence for Data Science and Advanced Cooperative Systems dealing with research problems of developing advanced methods and technologies for heterogeneous data analysis and complex cooperative system shaping.\
Considering strategic documents between the European Union and Republic of Croatia, the project DATACROSS is connected to “Partnership Agreement Republic of Croatia 2014HR16M8PA001-1.2” and “Operational Programme Competitiveness and Cohesion 2014-2020”.\
DATACROSS represents a milestone because it will strongly strengthen the research capacity of the Centre of Research Excellence by employing and improving more than 40 new young project researchers, which will enable the high intensity of top-level scientific research and the transfer of knowledge and technology to the economy. That will have significant positive effects on the strengthening of the Croatian research sector and the economy, as well as the society in general.
