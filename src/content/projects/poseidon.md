The POSEIDON project plans to research and develop a system for autonomous surveillance of protected maritime areas. This system will consist of an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV), which will be the central part of the system, on which other systems will be stationed. Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) will be used to search the area, which will be able to search a larger area from the air, and which will be able to be inductively charged while on the USV. The USV will also carry a quadruped robot and a robotic arm. The robotic arm will enable the quadruped robot to be transferred to the deck of another ship or to the shore so that the quadruped robot can explore areas that cannot be explored with aircraft. Communication between the elements of the system – the USV, the aircraft and the quadruped robot – must be efficient and secure in order to achieve real-time coordination of all elements. The project will consider appropriate communication technologies and protection of communication at the air interface, and address the challenges of real-time coordination of the system elements.
