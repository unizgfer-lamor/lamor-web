**RAiSE – Robotics, AI, and Computer Science for Engineering** connects education, science, and industry partners in Central and South-Eastern Europe to enhance teaching, research, and innovation in robotics, AI, and engineering. The network fosters interdisciplinary collaboration to tackle regional and global challenges, focusing on integrating AI and robotic solutions into real-world applications.

Key activities include student and teacher exchanges, joint research, co-supervision of theses, and an annual **Summer/Winter School on Robotics and AI**, which offers hands-on experience with cutting-edge technologies. RAiSE supports bilateral agreements between institutions, ensuring mutual recognition of learning outcomes, facilitating mobility, and preparing students for global careers.

The network includes 13 partners from 10 countries from faculties of electrical engineering, computer science, information and communication sciences, robotics and machine intelligence, medical & health technologies, and technical sciences.
