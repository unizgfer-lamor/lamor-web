The purpose of the project is to research and develop a number of innovative products, services and business models within different areas in the tourism value chain ecosystems that contribute to the green and digital transition and to solving key challenges in the tourism sector. The project partners plan to research and develop a number of products, services and business models that are planned to be commercialized after the project implementation, which include: i) an ecological smart mobile home ii) systems for automating the design, production and assembly of ecological smart mobile homes, iii) a digital service for physical protection and civil security in tourist destinations, iv) a system for monitoring and managing ecological smart mobile homes and v) a business model for electric mobility in tourist locations.
