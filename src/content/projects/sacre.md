SACRE aims to strengthen the employability and job retention of workers aged 55+ in manufacturing by equipping them with key competences in collaborative robotics, automation and digitalisation. The project will design and validate a competence-based curriculum, aligned with labour market needs and integrated into VET systems through micro-credentials, fostering inclusive and sustainable industrial transformation.

The project will analyse automation-driven changes in manufacturing and identify the impacted job roles, leading to a validated competence framework. It will then design and pilot a competence-based training programme for workers aged 55+, develop interactive online modules hosted on a learning management system, and structure EQF-aligned micro-credentials.
