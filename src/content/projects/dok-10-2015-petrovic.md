The development of PhD student Luka Fućek's scientific research career and the focus of the research he will conduct during his doctoral studies will focus on the development of systems for safe human-robot interaction in logistics applications for large, highly flexible warehouses. The research will be conducted as part of the Obzor2020 project SafeLog - Safe human-robot interaction in logistic applications for highly flexible warehouses.
