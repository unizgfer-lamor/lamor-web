The goal of the project is to improve the expertise and scientific competence, increase the level of acceptance of the new ideas in control and estimation of complex systems, and to give our own contribution to the development of those ideas, especially in deriving a unified methodological approach to the modeling, control and estimation of complex dynamical systems. Developed methods will be practically tested in: rail vehicles, wind power plants and Croatian electric power system.
